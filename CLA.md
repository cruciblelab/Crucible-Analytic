# Contributor Licence Agreement

Thank you for your interest in contributing to Crucible Analytic.

This agreement clarifies the intellectual property licence granted with
contributions from any person or entity. It does not change your rights
to use your own contributions for any other purpose.

## Why this exists

Two reasons, and the second is the one projects usually leave unsaid, so
it is said here first.

**It keeps the project's licensing decisions possible.** Crucible
Analytic is Apache-2.0 today. It may in future be offered under different
terms — a source-available licence, a commercial licence, or a
combination. Without this agreement, that decision would require the
written permission of every past contributor, which in practice means it
could never be made. If you would rather your contribution could never be
included in a version offered commercially, please do not sign; that is a
legitimate position and there is no hard feeling in it.

**It confirms you have the right to contribute what you are
contributing.** Most disputes about open source code are not about
licences at all — they are about an employee who contributed code their
employer owned.

## How to sign

Add a line to `CLA-SIGNATURES.md` in your first pull request, in the
format that file describes, and say in the pull request that you have
read and agree to this agreement.

Signing once covers your later contributions; you do not need to repeat
it for every pull request.
